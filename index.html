The Curator's Letter

A personal record, written for this day.

Happy 16th Birthday, Shubham.

I wanted to make something a little different for your birthday—something you could keep and return to whenever you felt like it.

Meeting you became an unexpected and special part of my Class 10 year. I appreciate your kind heart and the respectful way you treat people. Those qualities matter more than you might sometimes realise.

As you step into another year, I hope you learn to believe in yourself a little more. Don't let one difficult day, one mistake, or one doubtful thought decide how you see yourself. You have time to learn, grow, and discover so much about who you can become.

I hope this year brings you good memories, new opportunities, and plenty of reasons to feel proud of yourself. Keep your kindness, keep learning, and don't forget to give yourself the same patience you would give someone else.

A little effort to make your birthday special—and a small wish for the year ahead.

With good wishes,
